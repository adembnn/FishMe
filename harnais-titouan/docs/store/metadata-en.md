## Description (2064 characters)
```
Bredouille turns every freshwater fishing outing into a collectible card.

Snap your catch. The app suggests three plausible species among those found in your French département, and you always make the final call (or tap "None of these"). Your card appears: your cut-out photo, the species, the length you entered, a blurred location, the date.

No fish today? End the outing as "bredouille" (empty-handed) and you still get a card: "Eau calme" (calm water). Five of them earn the "Patience" card.

WHAT YOU GET
• One card per catch, with a finish that rises with local rarity
• Your département's bestiary, to complete at your own pace
• "Plan my outing": species plausible this month
• Releasing fish is rewarded; no ranking by size or rarity
• Works offline, right at the water's edge

RESPECTFUL AND HONEST
• No account, no ads, no analytics: your photos and journal stay on your iPhone.
• Location is optional, approximate (your département), never in the background.
• Rarity comes from the OFB's scientific survey data (electrofishing in rivers). It is not a measure of what anglers catch.
• The app never tells you whether you may keep a fish. Rules vary by département, waterway and year: check with your local fishing federation. When in doubt, release it.
• Children under 12 should fish with an adult close to the water.

BREDOUILLE PRO (OPTIONAL)
Unlimited cards, basin albums, holographic finishes and frames, journal export. Empty-handed outings and what is free today stay free.
• 24.99 EUR per year, with a 7-day free trial
• 3.99 EUR per month
Payment is charged to your Apple account at purchase confirmation; the free trial converts to a paid subscription at its end unless cancelled at least 24 hours before. The subscription renews automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. Manage or cancel in Settings > Apple Account > Subscriptions.
```
